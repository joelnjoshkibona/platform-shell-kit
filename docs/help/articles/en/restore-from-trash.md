Deleted records wait in the trash for 30 days before they go for good. Until then, they can be brought back exactly as they were, together with anything that was deleted with them.

::: shot route=trash
1 = main [role=tablist]  Show one module at a time
2 = main button[aria-label^="Filters"]  Filter by who deleted it, or when
3 = main button:has-text("Restore") || main button[aria-label^="Actions for"]  Bring it back (on a phone: in the row's actions)
:::

## Steps

1. Open **Trash** from the menu (under **System**).
2. Tap a module tab ① to see only its records, or open **Filters** ② to find it by who deleted it or when.
3. Tap **Restore** ③ on its row (on a phone, open the row's **⋯** actions first). It goes back where it was, with anything deleted along with it (a task with its subtasks).

> **Delete for good** cannot be undone: you are asked to type YES first.
