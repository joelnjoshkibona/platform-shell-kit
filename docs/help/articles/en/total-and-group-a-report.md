A report is a list with totals. You choose its columns, which ones stay pinned on the left, how each number column is totalled, and whether rows are gathered into groups.

::: shot route=report
1 = main button[aria-label^="Group rows"]  Group rows by a field, with subtotals
2 = main button:has-text("Columns")  Show, order, pin and total the columns
3 = main button[aria-label="Totals of every filtered row"]  Totals of every row the filters match
4 = main button[aria-label="Totals row under the table"]  A totals row for the rows on screen
:::

## Steps

1. Tap **Group** ① and choose a field, for example **By branch**. Each group shows how many rows it has and their subtotals; tap a group to fold or unfold it.
2. Tap **Columns** ② to tick the columns you want, move them up or down, pin them so they stay on the left while you scroll, and choose each number column's total: sum, average, count, smallest or largest.
3. Switch on **Totals of all rows** ③ to see, at the top, the totals of everything your filters match, on every page.
4. **Totals row** ④ adds a row under the table with the totals of the rows on screen.

> The table's header, pinned columns and totals row stay in place while you scroll. Tap **Save view** to keep all of this, for you or for your team.
