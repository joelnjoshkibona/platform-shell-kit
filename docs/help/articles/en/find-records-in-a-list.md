Every list in the product works the same way: search, quick tabs, filters and sort sit in the same places, and the list remembers them in its link, so you can copy the link and send someone the same view.

::: shot route=members
1 = header input[aria-label^="Search"] || header button[aria-label^="Search"]  Search this list (on a phone, tap the magnifier)
2 = main button[aria-label^="Filters"]  Filters: by any field, simple or advanced
3 = main button[aria-label^="Sort:"]  Sort: which field, and up or down
:::

## Steps

1. Type a word in the search box at the top ①. On a list page it searches that list; the list narrows as you type. On a phone, tap the magnifier first.
2. Many lists have quick tabs above the rows (for example **All**, **Cash**, **M-Pesa**). Tap one to see only that group; its number says how many rows it holds.
3. For anything more, open **Filters** ②. Choose the field, then what it should be. Each filter you add shows as a chip; tap the **×** on a chip to remove it.
4. Use **Sort** ③ to choose the order. Tap it again to turn the order around.

> The list keeps your search, filters and sort in its link. Press **Refresh** to load the newest rows without losing them.
