Fold rules

  1. 1The identity column (name or number, with avatar) never folds and keeps at least 40% of the row.
  2. 2One hero number stays right-aligned, tabular, at every width.
  3. 3Status folds to the right: a badge level with the title, the hero number directly under it, both top-anchored. Identity badges (SKU, record number) fold to the title line.
  4. 4Every other column folds, lowest priority first, into the meta line as A · B · C.
  5. 5The header shows only unfolded columns. Header click sorts; the Sort control still reaches folded columns.
  6. 6Folded fields fill the meta line in priority order while they fit on two lines. What doesn't fit is counted in a +N more chip that opens a stacked label/value strip under the row. The chevron and the row click always mean "open the record".
  7. 7Every list stacks the same way: toolbar · quick tabs (when the module has them, one scrollable row) · search · chips · column header · rows · footer. Coarse to fine: tabs cut big, search cuts small.
